LIVE STATUS NOTE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Keep `logs/status_now.md` current with two or three plain sentences that tell
someone checking in, with no context, where your thinking is right now:
- what this research is testing,
- what you are working on at this moment and why,
- what you have learned so far and what comes next.

Write it when you start, rewrite it whenever your plan or understanding changes
(for example after a key result, a failure, or a change of approach), and once
more before you finish. Replace the whole file each time; never append.

Use plain language. Do not include file paths, internal ids, commit hashes,
metric variable names, or Markdown formatting.

Example:
Testing whether giving a coding assistant the relevant repo files makes it ask
better clarifying questions. The first comparison looked promising but might be
luck, so I am rerunning it several times. Next I will check whether the
improvement holds across those reruns.
