RESEARCH STATE CONTRACT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

STATE.md is the compact handoff between phases. Read it before beginning each
phase. The generated sections describe pipeline status and expected artifacts;
do not rewrite or remove those sections. You own only the text between:

<!-- NEURICO_AGENT_NOTES_START:{{ phase_name }} -->
...
<!-- NEURICO_AGENT_NOTES_END:{{ phase_name }} -->

The `## Research Contract` section is owned by NeuriCo and regenerated on every
write, so edits to it are discarded. It restates the hypothesis, constraints,
success criteria, and expected outputs.

Treat the Research Contract as the anchor for every stage. Keep all planning,
implementation, experiments, and analysis aligned with the stated research
question and constraints. Do not change direction silently. If the evidence
contradicts the hypothesis, report that honestly rather than trying to support
it. When your work conflicts with the contract, record it under `Unresolved
issues` instead of diverging quietly.

At the end of every stage, update only your stage notes block, keeping
these five headings:
- Completed
- Key decisions and reasons
- Evidence/files
- Unresolved issues
- Next steps

Do not edit another stage's notes block. Each stage has its own section so
resource_finder findings remain available to later stages and cannot be
overwritten by experiment_runner.

Keep the notes concise enough to be useful as a handoff. If STATE.md does not
exist, create it with the marker pair above before starting Phase 1. Do not
claim a phase is complete until its expected artifacts exist on disk.

DIRECTION BUDGET
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

During Phase 1, enumerate the plausible research directions and score them
using evidence from the literature, relevance to the hypothesis, expected
information gain, and implementation feasibility. Keep only the top
{{ max_directions }} directions for implementation and experimentation. Record
the rejected directions and the reason for pruning in planning.md or STATE.md.
Do not expand the search space later unless new evidence invalidates the
ranking; if that happens, update the ranking and explain the change in STATE.md.
