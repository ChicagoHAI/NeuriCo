## Concordia AI, State of AI Safety in China (2026) — covers Jul 2025–Jun 2026
URL: https://aisafetychina.substack.com/p/state-of-ai-safety-in-china-2026 (announcement, Jul 14, 2026); PDF https://aisafetychina.substack.com/api/v1/file/6614941b-fef9-4a38-ad5e-00d39e4740be.pdf (also https://aisafetychina.com/assets/State-of-AI-Safety-in-China-2026.pdf). Verbatim passages mentioning Moonshot / Kimi:
- Executive summary: "Only five of ten leading foundation-model developers reported safety evaluation results alongside any release this past year, and none did so consistently; DeepSeek-R1's peer-reviewed Nature paper and Moonshot AI's Kimi K2 model card were the most detailed disclosures yet, but flagship follow-ups like DeepSeek-V4 and Kimi K2.5 were released with no safety evaluation results at all."
- §5.2.1 (p.72–73): "Of the ten companies, only five (Bytedance, DeepSeek, Meituan, Moonshot AI, and Z.AI) provided safety evaluation results alongside any model release during the year in question, and even those five did not do so consistently across releases." ... "Another relatively detailed example is Moonshot AI's Kimi K2. Moonshot AI worked with third-party tester Promptfoo on a wide range of safety evaluations spanning harmful content (e.g. hate speech, explicit material), criminal behavior (e.g. chemical and biological weapon development, IP theft), misinformation, privacy, and security (e.g. malicious code generation). Even in these cases, however, companies did not report comparably detailed disclosures for every new model release: the most recent releases from those companies (DeepSeek-V4 and Kimi K2.5) included no safety evaluation results at all."
- Table 5.1 rows: "Moonshot AI | Kimi K2 (Jul 2025) | No. Briefly describes seed prompts aimed at reducing "violence, fraud, and discrimination," with an automated prompt evolution pipeline to simulate jailbreaking attempts. | Safety evaluation performed with Promptfoo, covering: harmful, criminal, misinformation, privacy, security (each with multiple sub-categories)." ; "Kimi K2.5 (Feb 2026) | No | No" ; "Kimi K2.6 (Apr 2026) | No. Briefly mentions that the model has "enhanced safety awareness during extended research tasks." | No".
- §5.3 research: "DeepSeek, MoonShot, and MiniMax have no safety papers in our dataset since January 2025 at all. This may, in part, reflect stronger resource constraints..."
- Open-source stance (p.~2610): "Moonshot AI CEO YANG Zhilin (杨植麟) has said the company hopes to continue open sourcing over the long term but may not remain exclusively open, noting that some work, such as collaborations with certain partners, might not be released." (fn 232: Zhang Xiaojun podcast, Aug 2025)
- Industry commitments (§5.1.2): "Their voluntary safety commitments were first developed in December 2024, and a revised version in July 2025 modestly expanded their scope ... A new commitment on international engagement was added, and five new companies signed on." The 2026 report does NOT name the five new signatories; whether Moonshot is among them is UNVERIFIED. Agent commitments (Feb 2026) were "Signed by major phone manufacturers, cloud providers, and security firms"; the cloud-agent convention (Apr 2026) "was signed by eleven major Chinese cloud companies" — Moonshot not named.
- Frontier AI Risk Management Framework (Shanghai AI Lab & Concordia AI; and "Frontier AI Risk Management Framework in Practice" reports arXiv 2507.16534 / 2602.14457) is described as a research framework; no list of corporate adopters/signatories appears, and Moonshot is not mentioned in connection with it.
- AIIA/CAICT testing: "In November 2025, the program released cybersecurity misuse evaluations of 15 open source coding models" and "launched the AI Safety Benchmark 2.0 [Feb 2026] adding new categories for model deception, loss of control, dangerous-domain misuse, and agentic risks" — whether Kimi models were among the 15 is not stated in the passages read (UNVERIFIED).

## Concordia AI, State of AI Safety in China (2025) — covers May 2024–Jun 2025
URL: https://concordia-ai.com/research/state-of-ai-safety-in-china-2025/ ; PDF https://concordia-ai.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/State-of-AI-Safety-in-China-2025.pdf. Local: concordia-state-of-ai-safety-in-china-2025.pdf/.txt (99 pp)
- AIIA "AI Safety Commitments", Dec 2024: "which 17 Chinese firms endorsed, including LLM startups DeepSeek, Zhipu AI, MiniMax, and 01.AI, and large technology companies such as Alibaba, Baidu, ByteDance, and Huawei." Then (verbatim): "These commitments represent the strongest collective safety stance by Chinese industry to date, and have secured support from most major frontier AI developers. However, some leading LLM developers have not signed, including Moonshot AI and StepFun."
- Seoul Frontier AI Safety Commitments: Chinese signatories Zhipu (2024), MiniMax and 01.AI (early 2025) — Moonshot not a signatory.
- CEO statement (p.~54): "Moonshot AI CEO YANG Zhilin (杨植麟) acknowledged that while safety may not be the most "urgent" issue, it is "extremely important" and demands proactive preparation." (BAAI Conference, June 2024)
- Table 5.1 (2025): "Moonshot AI | Kimi k1.5 (January 2025) | Yes | Very brief safety mention: mitigates reward-hacking in RL | None mentioned." ; "Kimi-VL (April 2025) | Yes | Very brief mention of the need for alignment with human values | None mentioned."
- Safety research: "zero papers from DeepSeek, MiniMax, and Moonshot" over the year.
