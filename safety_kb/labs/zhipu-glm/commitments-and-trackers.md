## 1. Seoul Frontier AI Safety Commitments (May 21, 2024) — Zhipu signed; no framework published

Commitment text: "undertake to develop and deploy their frontier AI models and systems responsibly ... and to demonstrate how they have achieved this by publishing a safety framework focused on severe risks by the upcoming AI Summit in France."

- The v1.5 page (`concordia-frontier-ai-risk-management-framework-v1.5-page.md`) lists no corporate signatories; the Open-Weight framework (Sept 2026) is Z.AI's only documented participation (companion to Framework 2.0).

Family-level statements: "Kimi, GLM, MiMo, Qwen, Doubao, and other model families have also seen relatively fast Risk Index growth, mainly driven by capability growth"; biological: "Grok and GLM-family models have also crossed the Capability Yellow Line, but neither family has crossed t[he Risk Yellow Line]"; chemical: "The Kimi, GLM, and Grok families have also exceeded 70 [capability score]"; harmful manipulation: "In Safety Score, the lowest-scoring model is DeepSeek V4 Pro at 25.7. GLM and Kimi-family models have also fallen below 30, though newer versions have improved." Per-model GLM numbers are in interactive charts, not in the page text.

## 6. GLM-5.3 staged release (Aug 2026) — the lab's only release-gating action
- Primary: HF card ("Emergent Cyber Capability ..."), z.ai blog bundle text "The model weights of GLM-5.3 will be publicly available soon in two weeks." (`zai-blog-glm-5.3.md`), GLM-5.3 License security-review clause. The Z.ai X article "Preparing GLM-5.3 for Open Release: A Responsible Path to Cyber Defense" (https://x.com/Zai_org/article/2088280509474320693) returned HTTP 402 and could not be fetched — its content is known only through secondary quotes below (UNVERIFIED verbatim).
- Concordia (`concordia-aisafetychina-open-sourcing-2026.md`): "This was the first time a Chinese lab publicly justified a staged model release on safety grounds. The model was accessible in the company's API immediately, but Z.AI released the weights two weeks later to allow for "security hardening" to "limit potential offensive capability while preserving defensive value." ... Cybersecurity practitioners can access a cybersecurity-specific model version through a Beijing industry alliance. The model's bespoke license requires a security review for any Model-as-a-Service provider with over USD 10 billion revenue over 12 months." Quote attributed to Z.AI's announcement: "When the sharpest spear is locked in the hands of a few, the best shield must belong to everyone."
- The Batch (`deeplearning-ai-batch-glm-5.3-cyber-2026.md`): "Z.ai chose temporary guardrails rather than the permanent approach taken by OpenAI and Anthropic ... opted to release the model's weights only after two weeks of safety evaluation with vetted security partners." Also: "The company also studied and sought to prevent the model's attempts at reward hacking". Z.ai's own cyber numbers: CyberGym 84.5% (GLM-5.2 77.2%; Mythos 5 83.8%; GPT-5.6 Sol 83.6%); ExploitBench 54.4% (GLM-5.2 24.4%; Kimi K3 32.2; Mythos 5 78.0; GPT-5.6 Sol 76.5).
- Implicator (`implicator-zai-delays-glm-5.3-weights.md`, Aug 15, 2026): "holding the downloadable weights until around August 28, the first time it has delayed a GLM weight release"; "Its vulnerability ledger lists 2,436 findings across 269 open-source projects, with 53 disclosed and 2,383 still under embargo ... 107 critical flaws and 990 rated high"; "the most sensitive functions reserved for a trusted-access tier. Z.ai acknowledged that once the weights are public, it will no longer be able to control how people modify or use the model."
- CAISI confirms: "Z.ai publicly released the model's weights two weeks later." HF repo created 2026-08-25.
