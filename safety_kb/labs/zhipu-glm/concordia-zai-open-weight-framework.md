## Verbatim passages
> "Co-developed by Concordia AI and Z.AI, it adapts the full risk management lifecycle—from risk assessment to mitigation and governance—to the unique properties of open-weight models. It serves as a companion to the Frontier AI Risk Management Framework 2.0 co-developed by Shanghai AI Lab and Concordia AI."
> "Yet openness carries distinct, irreversible risks. Open-weight models can be fine-tuned to strip away safeguards, repurposed for malicious ends, and distributed beyond any central point that can take accountability. The major concerns are dual-use capability and unmonitored misuse."
> Structure (TOC): Risk Identification (Misuse Risks, Accident Risks); Risk Thresholds — "Deployment Environment (E)", "Threat Source (T)", "Enabling Capability (C)", "Societal Resilience (R)"; Risk Analysis (pre-development / pre-deployment / post-deployment); Risk Evaluation (Green / Yellow / Red zones); Risk Mitigation by stage; Risk Governance (Internal Governance; Transparency and Social Oversight).
> Risk Evaluation (p.14): "Developers should consider a range of deployment options. Staged release, structured access for vetted researchers and auditors, and controlled download for approved users preserve many of the benefits of openness ... The GLM-5.3 release illustrates this in practice: citing dual-use risks, access was structured so that vetted security partners evaluated the model in controlled settings first, with broader access and API availability following, and full weights were published only once safety evaluations and release preparations were complete [53]."
> Zones table: "Green Zone (Broadly Acceptable): Full open-weight release ... Requires baseline safety practices, model documentation, and post-release impact monitoring via available feedback channels. Yellow Zone (Tolerable): Restricted and/or staged release ... staged or gradual release, structured access for vetted researchers and external auditors, or gated access for specific use cases or hosting via API while weight release is evaluated. Red Zone (Unacceptable): Suspension of release ... whenever a model crosses domain-specific red lines pending re-evaluation. If an open-weight model is reclassified as Red Zone after release, mitigation must shift to ecosystem-level responses, as open-weight release cannot be reversed."
> "Current methods for making models refuse harmful requests or forget dangerous knowledge can often be removed with a few steps of fine-tuning [43]. ... Residual risk estimates for open-weight models therefore carry wider uncertainty than the same estimates for models kept behind a controlled API".
> References [53] "Z.AI (@Zai_org), GLM-5.3 Release Statement, X (formerly Twitter), Aug. 14, 2026." and [66] "Z.AI, GLM-5.3: Frontier Coding with Emergent Cyber Capabilities, Z.AI Blog, Aug. 14, 2026. https://z.ai/blog/glm-5.3".

## What it does not contain
No GLM-specific capability thresholds, no quantitative red lines bound to Z.AI models, no commitment by Z.AI to apply the zones to future GLM releases, no evaluation results. It should be read as Z.AI's closest published analogue to a frontier safety framework, but it is a joint think-tank document.
