## Phi-4 (14B) — technical report arXiv 2412.08905 + HF card
- URLs: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.08905 ; https://huggingface.co/microsoft/phi-4 (release date December 12, 2024; MIT). Raw: `arxiv-2412.08905-phi-4-technical-report.txt`, `phi-4-hf-readme.txt`.
- Approach (§7): "safety alignment in post-training, red-teaming, and automated testing and evaluations across dozens of RAI harm categories"; SFT + iterative DPO; 1-5% of DPO data is "hallucination and safety data".
- §7.1 RAI benchmarks (Table 10, in-house, GPT-4o-simulated multi-turn conversations; lower better except Grounding 0-5): phi-4 Grounding 4.619; 3P Content Harms DR1 0.121 (phi-3-14B 0.251, Llama-3-8B 0.373); Harmful Content Continuation DR3 0.036 (phi-3-14B 0.010, Llama-3-8B 0.013 — phi-4 is worse); Harmful Content Summarization DR3 0.102; Jailbreak DR1 0.073 (phi-3-14B 0.111, Llama-3-8B 0.13). Jailbreak set "consists of simulated conversations around child grooming, illegal persuasion, leaking of 100 words of guidelines, popular conspiracy, prejudice against real people, step-by-step illegal advice, and violence against real people".
- §7.2 Red teaming: "AIRT conducted a two-week red-teaming exercise ... emulating both average and adversarial users in single and multi-turn scenarios ... found that the behavior of phi-4 was similar to that of the phi-3 family, but identified several risky behaviors that were addressed by further rounds of safety post-training"; tested "jailbreaks, prompt encodings, and multi-turn attacks"; GCG adversarial suffixes generated on phi-3-medium "did not transfer to phi-4". "Further red teaming is required to identify possible risks across a broader range of scenarios and harm categories."
- Not disclosed: open-source benchmark scores (card says "multiple open-source safety benchmarks" were used but reports none), red-team findings, any dual-use (CBRN/cyber) capability evaluation, multilingual safety.

## Phi-4-reasoning / Phi-4-reasoning-plus (14B) — arXiv 2504.21318 + HF cards
- URLs: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.21318 ; https://huggingface.co/microsoft/Phi-4-reasoning ; .../Phi-4-reasoning-plus (release April 30, 2025). Raw: `arxiv-2504.21318-phi-4-reasoning.txt`, `phi-4-reasoning-hf-readme.txt`, `phi-4-reasoning-plus-hf-readme.txt`.
- Safety data: SFT prompts "sourced from [2, 39]" with Microsoft safety guidelines in the system prompt (removed at training time), covering "Knowledge Scope, Handling Complex and Sensitive Topics, Safety and Respectful Engagement, Confidentiality".
- §5.3 Safety evaluation: (1) Automated RAI Measurement Framework (LLM-as-user + LLM judge) for jailbreak success, harmful content, IP leakage — "Phi-4-reasoning shows minor regression compared to the base model Phi-4" (no numbers); (2) Toxigen discriminative (Table 11): Phi-4 toxic 72.6 / neutral 90.0; Phi-4-reasoning 86.7 / 84.7; Phi-4-reasoning-plus 77.3 / 90.5; GPT-4o 85.4 / 88.7; o3-mini 87.6 / 85.1 — "detecting toxicity without causing erasure is still a challenging problem".
- Stated limitations: LLM judges "have not yet been optimized for long, step-by-step traces"; evaluations "focus on measuring engagement and refusal in jailbreak scenarios and on classification-style toxic language detection"; open-form/benign-scenario harms not evaluated.
- HF card: "designed and tested for math reasoning only"; "elevated defect rate when responding to election-critical queries"; AIRT collaboration described qualitatively.
- Not disclosed: jailbreak ASR numbers, harmful-content defect rates, red-team findings, any capability-uplift evaluation.

## Phi-4-Mini (3.8B) and Phi-4-Multimodal (5.6B) — arXiv 2503.01743 + HF cards
- URLs: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.01743 ; https://huggingface.co/microsoft/Phi-4-mini-instruct (release February 2025). Raw: `arxiv-2503.01743-phi-4-mini-multimodal.txt`, `phi-4-mini-instruct-hf-readme.txt`.
- §5.1 Text safety (Azure AI Evaluation SDK, GPT-4o adversarial simulation; Defect Rate = fraction of harmful responses): Table 10 average Phi-4-Mini 3.75%, Phi-4-Multimodal 4%, Phi-3.5-mini 4%, GPT-4o-mini 4.25%, Llama-3.2-3B 5%, Qwen-2.5-3B 4.25% (Violence/Sexual/Self-Harm/Hateful). Table 11 with known jailbreaks prepended: Phi-4-Mini 1.25%, Phi-4-Multimodal 2.25%, Phi-3.5-mini 7.5%, GPT-4o-mini 5.25%, Llama-3.2-3B 8%, Qwen-2.5-3B 14%. Table 12 XSTest: IPRR (refusal of harmful; higher better) Phi-4-Mini 93.5%, Phi-4-Multimodal 92%, Phi-3.5-mini 87%, Llama-3.2-3B 92.5%, Qwen-2.5-3B 92%; VPRR (refusal of valid; lower better) 20.8% / 26.4% / 21.2% / 15.6% / 25.6%. Table 13 multilingual defect rates (avg over 7 Tier-1 languages): Phi-4-Mini 3.91%, Phi-4-Multimodal 5.06%, Phi-3.5-mini 6.31%, GPT-4o-mini 4.13%.
- §5.2 Audio safety: TTS-converted text safety data; acknowledged limits "voice-only" and "We did not train against audio-specific jailbreaks". Table 14 audio defect rate Phi-4-Multimodal 3.25% vs GPT-4o 1%. Speech fairness (WER by gender/age, 10 locales): no egregiously worse subgroup. Sensitive-attribute inference from voice: 27% of prompts (Qwen2-Audio 49%), reduced to 0.4% with a system prompt. Red team (independent group within Microsoft): "more susceptible to providing undesirable outputs when attacked with context manipulation or persuasive techniques ... mostly affecting French and Italian".
- §5.3 Vision safety (0-10 safer-is-higher): internal 7.96 (Phi-3.5-Vision 8.16, GPT4-V 8.55); RTVLM 6.39 (GPT4-V 6.81); VLGuard 8.91 (GPT4-V 8.90).
- Not disclosed: red-team sample sizes, CBRN/cyber capability evaluation, audio jailbreak results.

## Cross-cutting
- Red teaming is always Microsoft AIRT (internal, independent of product team); no external red team or AISI involvement for Phi.
- Public safety benchmarks actually scored: Toxigen (reasoning paper), XSTest, RTVLM, VLGuard (mini paper). HarmBench, StrongREJECT, WMDP etc. are not reported for Phi. Methods paper: "Phi-3 Safety Post-Training: Aligning Language Models with a 'Break-Fix' Cycle" (arXiv 2407.13833; used PyRIT) — referenced, not fetched.
- The 2025 RAI Transparency Report adds that "every Phi model released" in 2024 was red-teamed by AIRT.
