# Amazon Nova technical reports, FMSF evaluation reports and AI Service Cards

Fetched: 2026-10-19. Raw text under `safety_kb/sources/amazon/`.

## 1. "The Amazon Nova Family of Models: Technical Report and Model Card" (Nova Micro/Lite/Pro/Canvas/Reel)
- URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.12103 (also cdn.amazon.science nova-tech-report-20250317-0810.pdf); raw: `arxiv-2506.12103-nova-family-technical-report.txt`. Dated Dec 2024 (arXiv v1 Jun 2025).
- §5 Responsible AI: eight RAI dimensions; §5.3 RAI evaluation uses "publicly available benchmarks like BOLD, RealToxicityPrompts, and MM-SafetyBench" plus proprietary "dynamically updating" multilingual/multimodal adversarial sets. No numbers published.
- §5.4 Red teaming: internal (">300 distinct techniques" — Figure 4 taxonomy: prompt injections 40, jailbreak 24, multilingual 33, context-based 32, persuasion 119, obfuscations 59 = 307 attacks), external (ActiveFence: ">9,700 adversarial prompts, distributed over 20 categories"; Deloitte/Gryphon Scientific: "panel of 30 questions" on bio-weapons knowledge; Gomes Group at CMU: 39 hazardous chemicals + 362 common chemicals, NFPA-diamond evaluation of 1,810 prompts, aggregation-attack scenarios; Nemesys Insights: R/N red teaming, "8 subject matter experts ... 2-team x 2-scenario design ... 6-hour planning cycle" on Cobalt-60 and HEU acquisition scenarios), automated (FLIRT in-context red teaming).
- Not disclosed: any pass/fail numbers from the external red teams; CBRN uplift conclusions beyond qualitative text; no FMSF-style threshold evaluation (pre-dates framework).

## 2. Amazon Nova Premier Technical Report and Model Card (Apr/May 2025)
- URL: https://cdn.amazon.science/e5/e6/ccc5378c42dca467d1abe1628ec9/amazon-nova-premier-technical-report-and-model-card.pdf; raw: `nova-premier-technical-report-and-model-card.txt`.
- §3.2.1: public RAI benchmarks "BOLD, WILDCHAT non-toxic, and StrongReject" plus internal RAI benchmarks (safety, privacy, veracity, transparency); automated red teaming with "enhanced ... FLIRT framework" (multilingual, multimodal); external: ActiveFence and Innodata (hate speech, political misinformation, extremism), Gomes Group (chemical synonym vulnerabilities), Nemesys (nuclear-facility threat scenarios: insider sabotage, cyberattack, plutonium theft), Deloitte (bio knowledge).
- §3.2.2 FMSF evaluations: CBRN via WMDP, ProtocolQA, BioLP-Bench + Nemesys red teaming/uplift ("remains below the critical threshold"); Cyber via SECURE, CTIBench, CyberMetric + Cybench + internal security-team red teaming ("below the critical threshold"); AI R&D via RE-Bench + internal simulations reviewed by METR ("does not meet the critical threshold").
- No numbers in the tech report itself; see item 3.

## 3. "Evaluating the Critical Risks of Amazon's Nova Premier under the Frontier Model Safety Framework" (Krishna et al., Amazon Nova Responsible AI, arXiv 2507.06260, 7 Jul 2025)
- URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.06260; raw: `arxiv-2507.06260-nova-premier-critical-risks.txt` (10 pp.).
- CBRN automated (Fig.1, Premier vs Pro): WMDP-Bio 0.84 (+2pp), WMDP-Chem 0.66 (+3pp), ProtocolQA-MCQ 0.48 (Pro 0.34), BioLP-Bench 0.23 (Pro 0.10). WMDP-Bio 1,273 items, WMDP-Chem 408, ProtocolQA 108, BioLP 800 samples.
- CBRN human-centric (Nemesys "Exploratory Critical-Capability Threshold Analysis"): 120 uplift-indicator prompts (60 synthetic biology, 60 microbiology/delivery), 3 SME blind scorers, 0-10 rubric. "Nova Premier refused outright on 17% of prompts and issued partial deflections on another 18%. It answered directly on 78 prompts, including 44 that experts had tagged 'Definitely Should Not Respond', yielding a 61% false-negative rate. Direct answers were highly accurate (mean 8.7/10) but only moderately complete (6.6/10) and carried an average safety risk of 4.2/10". Nemesys: "routinely displayed an emergent capability that is necessary but not sufficient to breach the CBRN critical threshold".
- Cyber: aggregated public Q&A sets + 40 public CTFs (Cybench); "clear improvement on aggregated knowledge questions but remains safe with respect to mean CTF solve rate" (figure only; Nova 2 Lite paper later gives Premier CTF 7.5% guided / 2.5% unguided). Qualitative traces: content filters blocked exploit payloads (pickle), model stalls before ROP chains. Human-centric: internal Amazon cyber teams — guided malware generation (MITRE ATT&CK, EDR evasion) and zero-day discovery; "meets the safety requirements".
- AI R&D: RE-Bench traces (embedding repair, training-pipeline optimization, restricted MLM) — partial successes, "did not conduct end-to-end verification"; multi-agent simulations (100 rounds): "Nova Premier exhibited behavior flagged for review in 32.2% of agent instances and triggered review flags in 56% of scenarios. Nova Pro ... 17.4% ... 33%" — "none of the behaviors observed crossed the critical thresholds". METR re-scored six RE-Bench transcripts and simulation logs: "each solution was either non-functional or materially under-performing"; "no evidence of hidden capabilities masked by refusal behaviour"; noted "runtime policy filters were active during these trials; they therefore recommended repeating certain probes on an evaluation endpoint with safeguards disabled to rule out 'sandbagging' artefacts".
- Not disclosed: CTF solve-rate numbers, SECURE/CTIBench/CyberMetric scores, whether safeguards were disabled for any capability eval (apparently not), uplift-study design for cyber, Nemesys scoring rubric.

## 4. "Evaluating Nova 2.0 Lite model under Amazon's Frontier Model Safety Framework" (Krishna et al., arXiv 2601.19134, 27 Jan 2026)
- URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.19134; raw: `arxiv-2601.19134-nova-2-lite-critical-risks.txt` (9 pp.). Also covers Nova Act ("powered by a custom version of Nova 2.0 Lite").
- CBRN automated (best of low/medium/high reasoning): WMDP-Chem 0.71 (Premier 0.66, Pro 0.63); WMDP-Bio ~0.82 (Premier 0.84); ProtocolQA 0.49 (Premier 0.48); BioLP-Bench 0.24; **VCT 0.29** (Premier 0.30, Pro 0.15; expert baseline 22.1%). New "Chem-Vision" set: 105 chemicals (dangerous/safe/contested) with structure images.
- CBRN human-centric: Nemesys "large-scale, independent uplift study ... nearly 800 participants"; "remains below the overall CBRN threshold" but "identified a meaningful performance uplift in the radiological domain, where model assistance improved the scientific and technical components of attack planning" -> Amazon "deployed additional safeguards by enriching our safety filters and augmenting monitoring". Effect sizes not published.
- Cyber: CyberMetric, SECURE-CWET, CTIBench ">85%"; Cybench 40 tasks: Nova Pro 2.5% unguided / 7.5% guided; Premier 2.5 / 7.5; Nova 2.0 Lite 10.0 / **15.0%** ("7.5% uplift"). Human-centric: Hack The Box labs via Kali EC2 + MCP agent, three phases (autonomous, scenario, HITL); "marginal improvement (within 10% of human baseline) in cloud security testing"; "did not cross the critical threshold".
- AI R&D: RE-Bench qualitative; METR statement: "we believe this model does not cross the Automated AI R&D Critical Capability Threshold ... based primarily on evaluation results that Amazon shared with us ... Amazon also provided information to help us rule out severe under-elicitation and to upper-bound the delay from internal use of the model to its public deployment."
- Not disclosed: RE-Bench scores, participant-level uplift numbers, which safeguards were on during Cybench, Nova 2 Pro/Omni results (paper promises "safety reports of other follow-up models ... as they are made generally available" — none found as of 2026-10-19).

## 5. AWS AI Service Cards (Nova 2 Lite, Nova 2 Sonic; "current as of December 2, 2025")
- URLs: https://docs.aws.amazon.com/ai/responsible-ai/nova-2-lite/overview.html, https://docs.aws.amazon.com/ai/responsible-ai/nova-2-sonic/overview.html; raw: `aws-service-card-nova-2-lite.txt`, `aws-service-card-nova-2-sonic.txt`. Nova Premier / Nova 2 Pro / Nova 2 Omni service-card URLs returned 404 on 2026-10-19.
- Nova 2 Lite numbers: harmlessness — "proprietary datasets containing 6.4k prompts designed to elicit harmful responses ... correctly provides safe responses to over 98%"; toxicity — "8.5K prompts ... end-to-end guardrails provide safe responses to over 95%"; fairness — "1.4k prompts that attempt to solicit biased responses ... safe responses to 95.8%". External benchmarks named: "WILDCHAT non-toxic and WMDT [sic — WMDP, link wmdp.ai]". CBRN: "we see no indications that Amazon Nova 2 Lite increases access to information about ... CBRN threats". "Independent Red Teaming Network: Consistent with our Frontier AI Safety Commitments ... we leverage red teaming firms ... We also work with specialized firms and academics to red team our models for ... Cybersecurity and CBRN capabilities." CSAM: Bedrock hash-matching/classifiers, NCMEC reporting. Reasoning content is "[REDACTED]" to customers.
- Nova 2 Sonic numbers: "more than 96% of harmful prompts" safely handled ("improvement of more than 61% relative to Amazon Nova 1 Sonic"); voice-cloning prompts deflected "100% of the time"; toxicity 8.5K prompts ">95%".
- Not disclosed: benchmark scores for the external sets, red-team vendor results, jailbreak rates, prompt-injection rates for the web-grounding tool, any FMSF numbers.

## 6. AWS Responsible AI pages
- https://aws.amazon.com/ai/responsible-ai/ (raw `aws-responsible-ai-page.txt`) and Nova responsible-use guide (https://docs.aws.amazon.com/nova/latest/userguide/responsible-use.html, raw `aws-nova-responsible-use.txt`): eight dimensions (fairness, explainability, privacy & security, safety, controllability, veracity & robustness, governance, transparency); content-moderation guideline list; no evaluation numbers.

## Cross-cutting gaps
No Amazon document publishes jailbreak ASR, agentic-harm or prompt-injection metrics, CoT/monitoring details, or security-audit results; FMSF reports exist for Premier and Nova 2 Lite only; "third-party" involvement is mostly review of Amazon-run evaluations; the Nemesys 61% false-negative finding on Premier is the most candid disclosure.
