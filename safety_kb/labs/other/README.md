## Cross-cutting observations
- Thresholds: Microsoft (qualitative 4-level tables), Amazon (one qualitative critical threshold per domain), G42 (qualitative + DML/SML tiers) and Magic (coding-benchmark trigger) define them; NVIDIA, Cohere and NAVER 2.0 do not.
- Published model-level frontier evaluations: Amazon only (two FMSF reports). Microsoft and the others publish no capability-threshold results for any model.
- Named third parties: Amazon (Nemesys, METR review, ActiveFence, Innodata, Deloitte, Gomes Group), Microsoft (AIRT internal; CAISI MoU and UK AISI agreement announced 2026 with no results), Cohere (NIST, Humane Intelligence), G42 (framework co-authored with METR/SaferAI), Magic (METR advice). No AISI/CAISI results are public for any of them.
- Common gaps: no uplift-study effect sizes, no security-audit outcomes, no incident disclosures, no pre-mitigation vs post-mitigation numbers (except Phi-4-mini jailbreak tables and MAI-Thinking-1 relative reductions).
