# Alibaba Qwen team (Qwen3 / Qwen3.5 / Qwen3.6 / Qwen3.8 open-weight family)

Fetched: 2026-10-19

Qwen is the open-weight LLM/VLM family of Alibaba Group's Qwen team (Alibaba Cloud / Tongyi Lab), first open-weighted in August 2023 ("Our Approach" page). Current open generations as of 2026-10-19: Qwen3 (Apr 2025, tech report arXiv 2505.09388; dense 0.6B-32B, MoE 30B-A3B/235B-A22B; the "-2507" Instruct/Thinking refreshes Jul-Aug 2025; Qwen3-Next-80B-A3B Sep 2025; Qwen3-VL/Omni/Coder Sep 2025-Jan 2026), Qwen3.5 (Feb-Mar 2026, 0.8B/2B/4B/9B/27B dense, 35B-A3B/122B-A10B/397B-A17B MoE, native vision, 201 languages), Qwen3.6 (Apr 2026, 35B-A3B and 27B), and Qwen3.8 (Aug 2026, 27B dense under Apache-2.0; 2.4T-A95B "Max-class" under a custom Qwen3.8-Max license; Flash-Next 125B-A6B under "Qwen Community License 1.0"). Qwen3.7 Plus/Max, Qwen3.5-Plus/Flash, Qwen3.6-Max and Qwen3.8-Max/Flash are hosted-only (no HF repos). Documentation lives on Hugging Face (https://huggingface.co/Qwen), ModelScope, GitHub (https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3, /Qwen3.5, /Qwen3.6, /Qwen3.8, /Qwen3Guard), the JS-rendered blog https://qwen.ai/blog (legacy https://qwenlm.github.io/blog/) and arXiv. Safety documentation is thin: the only Qwen document with safety tables is the **Qwen3Guard Technical Report** (arXiv 2510.14276, Oct 2025, guard models 0.6B/4B/8B + Qwen3-4B-SafeRL); the only lab-level safety-process text is Alibaba Group's "AI Governance / Model Governance" web pages. No Qwen3.5/3.6/3.8 technical report exists (only blogs and cards), and no model card for any chat model contains a safety section. Third parties (Shanghai AI Lab, FLI, Fudan, Promptfoo, academic groups) supply essentially all numeric safety evidence on the family.

## Safety training and mitigations

What the documents say, verbatim where precise:

- **Pretraining data** - Qwen3 report Sec. 3.1: annotation system "annotating over 30 trillion tokens across multiple dimensions such as educational value, fields, domains, and safety. These detailed annotations support more effective data filtering". Alibaba governance page: "a multi-stage filtering process to all collected training data, combining rule-based systems, AI-driven classifiers, and human sampling to remove: Illegal, violent, pornographic, or hate-related content; Misinformation and content promoting harm; Personally identifiable information (PII)"; "Dynamic harmful-source filtering: We maintain an active monitoring system that identifies and blocks potentially harmful websites".
- **Post-training (SFT / DPO / GRPO)** - Alibaba governance page: "Our post-training pipeline typically includes techniques such as Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT), Direct Preference Optimization (DPO), and Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)"; safe SFT corpus "does not simply reject problematic inputs. Instead, it provides appropriate, constructive responses ... for malicious prompts, manipulative framing, or false premises, it explicitly identifies the issue"; GRPO "Enhanced Reward Model: The reward model is extended ... to also evaluate safety and the presence of positive, constructive guidance"; "Diversified Risk-Aware Training Data: The GRPO training dataset is deliberately augmented with a wide range of risk scenarios and adversarial prompts". Qwen2.5 report: Online RL reward criteria include "Harmlessness: The model must prioritize user safety by avoiding any content that could lead to illegal, immoral, or harmful behavior" and "Debiasing". Qwen3 report Sec. 4.4: General RL "reward system covering over 20 distinct tasks" - safety is not listed; hallucination-reduction reward for RAG.
- **Reasoning-mode safety** - governance page: "Alibaba constructs a safe reasoning corpus: the thinking process itself avoids unsafe assumptions or harmful reasoning, and—when faced with malicious instructions—actively analyzes user intent and formulates safe response strategies." and "Starting from Qwen 3 ... To defend against adversarial behaviors such as jailbreak attacks, the system incorporates multi-dimensional safety reflection during inference." (Third-party data cut the other way: Shanghai AI Lab Table 14 shows Qwen3-8b thinking-mode jailbreak ASR 32.2% vs 13.3% non-thinking.)
- **Multilingual safety data** - governance page: "Creating universal safety-aligned corpora based on shared human values applicable across all languages; Developing locally adapted safety corpora that reflect the cultural context, religious beliefs, and social norms of each target language region."
- **Guard models** - Qwen3Guard-Gen/Stream (0.6B/4B/8B), 1.19M labelled samples, tri-class safe/controversial/unsafe, nine categories incl. "Politically Sensitive Topics" and "Jailbreak"; released Apache-2.0; also the engine of the "Alibaba Cloud AI Guardrails service" (blog). Stream variant enables token-level intervention (CARE).
- **Safety RL with guard reward** - Qwen3-4B-SafeRL: GSPO on WildJailbreak prompts with hybrid reward r = min(-10, WorldPM) if unsafe, min(-5, WorldPM) if refusal, else WorldPM (Q3G Eq. 2); explicitly designed to avoid "overly simplistic or blanket refusals". Only the 4B model was released this way; no SafeRL variant exists for other sizes or for Qwen3.5+.
- **System-prompt policies** - governance page: "By embedding structured, executable safety policies directly into the system prompt, the model is provided with consistent and persistent behavioral constraints." No actual system prompt is published (FLI: "No transparency on system prompts for the deployed models").
- **Service-level moderation (Alibaba Cloud Model Studio)** - "Standard Q&A Repository" of pre-approved answers; "content safety engine ... all modalities (text, image, video, audio) ... real-time protection for both model inputs and outputs—supporting both streaming and non-streaming generation modes"; image/video detection includes "recognition of specific public figures".
- **Constitutional-style methods** - not mentioned in any Qwen document (Concordia notes constitutional AI is mentioned by DeepSeek, not Alibaba).
- **Model-weight / tamper-resistance** - not mentioned by the lab; Dealign.ai claims Qwen3.5-122B refusal is "a deep geometric basin" resistant to abliteration (UNVERIFIED).

## Monitoring, frameworks and governance

- **Post-release monitoring**: only for hosted services - "Providing access through a managed API allows real-time monitoring, rule enforcement, rapid response to emerging threats, and continuous safety updates" (Why Open Weight page); "Qwen model rules are enforced through a combination of automated checks, human review, and reports from users ... access to Qwen model services may be suspended or ended" (Model Governance page). Nothing for open weights beyond the Apache-2.0 licence (no AUP). FLI: "No public monitoring and control plan found for internal deployment."
- **Incident reporting**: no lab channel or published incident log. Legal duty under Art. 14 of the 2023 Generative AI Interim Measures and the Deep-Synthesis Provisions to report incidents to the CAC (FLI grading sheet). One incident is on record via FLI (ROME agent reverse-SSH / crypto-mining during an RL run, caught by cloud firewall).
- **Usage policy**: "Prohibited Uses Of Qwen" on alibabagroup.com (CSAM, privacy/biometric ID, violence/terror/self-harm, fraud, exploitation, workplace/education emotion inference; high-risk-use human review; minors protections). Binds services, not weights.
- **Frontier-safety / responsible-scaling policy**: **none exists.** FLI Summer 2026: "The company has no published framework; its rating largely reflects the Chinese regulatory environment"; "No public-facing existential risk strategy found"; "No public-facing quantitative safety plan found." Lab's own words on the subject are limited to Q3G's conclusion ("AI safety is a complex and ongoing challenge ... it is not a complete solution") and six corporate principles.
- **Chinese commitments**: CAICT/AIIA "AI Safety Commitments" (Dec 2024) - Alibaba is a named signatory (AGORA; AI Frontiers; Carnegie). WAIC 2025 / CnAISDA second round - Alibaba's participation UNVERIFIED (DeepSeek noted absent; no list found). Shanghai AI Lab + Concordia AI "Frontier AI Risk Management Framework v1.0" (July 2025) - a guideline with no signatories; Alibaba has not announced adoption; its technical report evaluated Qwen3-8B/32B without Alibaba involvement. Seoul Frontier AI Safety Commitments (2024) - not signed.
- **TC260 / CAC standards**: Alibaba co-drafted the binding AI-generated-content labelling standard (FLI). No Qwen document cites the TC260 "AI Safety Governance Framework", GB/T 45654-2025, or the 2023 Interim Measures. Model filing (备案): Tongyi Qianwen services were registered in 2023 (UNVERIFIED filing numbers for current models). ISO/IEC 42001 certification of Alibaba Cloud (CQC, 2024-09-12, via Concordia).
- **Governance bodies**: "cross-functional Technology Ethics Review Committee" and "dual-track governance system: algorithmic mechanism review and technology ethics review" (governance page); CTO leads the technology ethics committee (2024 ESG report via Concordia). FLI credits "a two-layered safety strategy spanning the Qwen model-level team and the Alibaba security team" but finds no whistleblower policy.
- **Safety research footprint**: Concordia 2025 - Alibaba had the most dedicated frontier-safety researchers among Chinese developers (four anchor authors; interpretability, scalable oversight; SciSafeEval with Zhejiang University); Alibaba released residual SAEs for Qwen3/Qwen3.5 bases (Apr 2026).
