- Fetched: 2026-10-19
- Local sources (`safety_kb/sources/alibaba-qwen/`): `agora-eto-caict-ai-safety-commitments.md` (https://agora.eto.tech/instrument/1831), `carnegie-chinese-firms-ai-promises-2025.md` (https://carnegieendowment.org/research/2025/01/deepseek-and-other-chinese-firms-converge-with-western-companies-on-ai-promises), `ai-frontiers-is-china-serious-about-ai-safety.md` (https://ai-frontiers.org/articles/is-china-serious-about-ai-safety), `aisafetychina-frontier-ai-risk-management-framework.md` (https://aisafetychina.substack.com/p/shanghai-ai-lab-and-concordia-ai, 2025-07-31), `aigl-state-of-ai-safety-in-china-2026.md`, `fli-ai-safety-index-summer-2026.txt` (regulatory rows), `concordia-state-of-ai-safety-in-china-2025.txt`.

## 1. CAICT / AIIA "Artificial Intelligence Safety Commitments" (人工智能安全承诺), December 2024 - Alibaba SIGNED
- AGORA (ETO) record: "Proposed 2024-12-01 | Enacted 2024-12-01 ... Signed by major companies, including Alibaba, Baidu, Huawei, Tencent, and others". Summary of commitments (AGORA): "Calls for the establishment of safety teams and risk management mechanisms ... Mandates safety and security testing for AI models, prioritizing evaluations in critical domains. Implements data security measures ... Enhances infrastructure security through regular penetration tests and incident response protocols. Requires transparency in AI model capabilities, limitations, and risk disclosures. Advances research in AI safety and security, addressing potential biases and risks in frontier applications."
- Commitment text (AGORA unofficial copy, verbatim excerpts): "Commitment I: Establish safety teams or organizational structures and build safety and security risk management mechanisms. Designate leader responsible for AI safety and security, establish specialized teams to conduct AI risk assessments and safety governance within the enterprise. Proactively define realistic safety risk baselines, adopt appropriate safety measures for open-source initiativ[es] ..." "Commitment II: Conduct safety and security testing for AI models to enhance the performance, safety, and reliability. Through dedicated simulation and red-teaming experts to rigorously test AI models prior to their release or update. For large models in particular, prioritize safety and reliability evaluations focusing on their general understanding, reasoning, and decision-making capabilities [in critical domains such as education, medicine, finance] ..."
- AI Frontiers (2025): "In December 2024, China's Artificial Intelligence Industry Alliance, a part of CAICT, released safety commitments signed by 17 major Chinese AI companies, including DeepSeek, Alibaba, and Tencent. Notably, these commitments closely mirror the Frontier AI Safety Commitments made by AI developers around the world at the Seoul AI Summit." Carnegie (Jan 2025): "sixteen new Chinese companies join the sixteen signatories of the Seoul Commitments".
- FLI Summer 2026 (p.17): the Chinese signatories "receive only an increase in score for 'signing' relevant safety commitments, which one reviewer dismissed as 'generic and used only as a reference.'"
- Alibaba did **not** sign the May 2024 Seoul Frontier AI Safety Commitments (Carnegie: "Only one Chinese frontier AI company signed onto the commitments: Zhipu.ai").

## 3. Shanghai AI Lab + Concordia AI "Frontier AI Risk Management Framework v1.0" (WAIC, July 2025)
- It is a guideline ("This framework serves as a guideline for general-purpose AI model developers"), six stages (risk identification, thresholds, analysis, evaluation, mitigation, governance), with red/yellow lines for cyber offense, biological threats, persuasion/manipulation, loss of control. It is **not a signed commitment**; no company signatory list exists. "We call on frontier AI developers, policymakers, and stakeholders to adopt compatible risk management frameworks."
- Alibaba has not published any adoption of it (nothing on alibabagroup.com or qwen.ai; FLI: "The company has no published framework"). The companion technical report evaluated Qwen3-8B/32B (see `third-party-shanghai-ai-lab-frontier-risk-report.md`).

## 4. Lab-published frontier-safety framework: NONE
- FLI Summer 2026: "Alibaba: No public-facing existential risk strategy found." "No public monitoring and control plan found for internal deployment." "Publish a full safety framework and governance structure. The company has no published framework".
- The only lab-published governance material is the alibabagroup.com AI Governance pages (six principles, lifecycle safety description, prohibited-use policy) - see `alibaba-ai-governance-pages.md` - and (per Concordia) the Alibaba/CESI Chinese-language "Large-Model Technology Development and Governance Practice Report" chapter 6 (April/July 2025, WeChat, not fetched).

## 5. Regulatory / standards references found
- Generative AI Interim Measures (2023): FLI grading sheet "Alibaba | Qwen3.5 | Article 14 of the Interim Measures for the Management of Generative Artificial Intelligence Services (2023) requires providers to promptly remove or disable unlawful AI-generated content, retrain or adjust their models where necessary, and report both the incident and any user misuse to relevant authorities." Deep-Synthesis Provisions (2023) similarly cited.
- Model registration / 备案: Alibaba's Tongyi Qianwen service was among early-registered generative AI services after the Measures took effect in August 2023 (search snippet, pedaily 2023-09; the specific filing number for Qwen3/3.5 was not found - UNVERIFIED). Concordia 2026 summary: closed-door "private pre-deployment evaluations conducted via China's mandatory algorithm registry" are not public.
- Standards: FLI: "Alibaba is among the entities that have drafted the Cybersecurity technology—Labeling method for content generated by artificial intelligence, which is the only binding national standard that requires generative AI services to label AI-generated content". GB/T 45654-2025 ("Basic security requirements for generative AI service", TC260): FLI credits DeepSeek among drafters; Alibaba's role not found. TC260 "AI Safety Governance Framework" 1.0 (Sep 2024) / 2.0 (2025, per Concordia 2026 summary "introducing 'derivative risks' and circuit breakers"): no Qwen document references it.
- ISO/IEC 42001: Concordia 2025 - Alibaba Cloud received the first CQC AI management system certificate (2024-09-12).
- Local rules: FLI - Shanghai/Shenzhen AI regulations "not directly applicable to Z.ai, DeepSeek, and Alibaba" (Alibaba is Zhejiang-based).

## 6. Post-release monitoring and incident handling
- Alibaba governance page: service-level moderation on Model Studio (content safety engine on inputs/outputs, "Standard Q&A repository"), enforcement "through a combination of automated checks, human review, and reports from users". No public incident-reporting channel, no bug-bounty for model safety, no published incident log.
- FLI records one incident (ROME agent, reverse SSH tunnel + crypto-mining during an RL run, caught by the cloud firewall; "properly addressed").
- Why Open Weight page: hosted-only for "most capable models" to allow "real-time monitoring, rule enforcement, rapid response to emerging threats" - but Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B (Aug 2026) was released openly anyway.
