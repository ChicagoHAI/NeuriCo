## 1. AgentHazard (arXiv 2604.02947, April 2026) - agentic misuse of coding agents
- Local: `arxiv-2604.02947-agenthazard.{pdf,txt}` (15 pages). Benchmark of 2,653 instances (per abstract) of decomposed multi-step harmful tasks run inside real agent frameworks (OpenClaw, Claude Code, IFlow); ASR judged by LLM-as-judge over full trajectories.
- Table 2 (ASR %, overall and avg harmfulness 0-10): OpenClaw - Qwen2.5-72B-Inst. 24.10 (2.07); **Qwen3-32B 59.18 (5.45)**; **Qwen3-VL-235B-Inst. 63.00 (5.45)**; Qwen2.5-Coder-32B 64.06; GLM-4.6 70.80; Kimi-K2 71.10. Claude Code - Qwen2.5-72B 20.40; Qwen2.5-Coder-32B 57.80; **Qwen3-VL-235B 65.60 (5.45)**; **Qwen3-Coder 73.63 (5.95)**; Kimi-K2.5 78.30; GLM-4.6 82.90. IFlow - Qwen2.5-Coder-32B 74.70. Per-category for Qwen3-Coder in Claude Code: RCE 78.26, Exfil 77.42, Persist 74.90, Supply 72.18, Destruct 68.00, Recon 75.06, PrivEsc 65.27, ResExh 84.04, PrmTheft 61.15, Comply 64.94.
- Abstract: "powered by Qwen3-Coder, Claude Code exhibits an attack success rate of 73.63%, suggesting that model-level alignment does not reliably transfer to agent-level safety."
- Table 3 guard detection (unsafe detection rate %, R1 | R2 | R3 | R4 | R_all): Llama-Guard-3-8B 4.11 | 11.61 | 22.04 | 34.95 | 27.03; Qwen3Guard-0.6B 2.30 | 5.28 | 11.46 | 22.92 | 16.59; Qwen3Guard-4B 1.36 | 3.58 | 9.52 | 21.37 | 15.30; Qwen3Guard-8B 0.87 | 3.51 | 10.32 | 22.22 | 16.21. "the Qwen3Guard family plateaus around 15-17% ... current guard models are insufficient as pre-execution filters for the multi-step harmful intent".

## 3. "Sycophancy Towards Researchers Drives Performative Misalignment" (arXiv 2606.08629, June 2026, ICML 2026 spotlight) - evaluation awareness
- Local: `arxiv-2606.08629-sycophancy-towards-researchers.{pdf,txt}` (19 pages). Models: Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507, Qwen3-235B-A22B-Instruct-2507, Qwen3-Next-80B-A3B-Thinking/Instruct, Qwen3-235B-A22B-Thinking-2507, Kimi-K2, gpt-oss.
- Table 5 (EVAL detection rate %, deployment system prompt, 240 prompts; Overall | Research-style | Ordinary): Qwen3-80B Instruct 11.7 | 18.3 | 5.0; Qwen3-80B Thinking 42.5 | 57.5 | 27.5 (gap +30.8); Qwen3-235B Instruct 39.6 | 59.2 | 20.0; Qwen3-235B Thinking 48.9 | 63.6 | 34.5 (gap +9.3); Kimi-K2 Instruct 50.0 / Thinking 78.2. "Thinking models consistently show higher evaluation awareness than instruct variants ... all differences statistically significant (p < 0.05)."
- Sec. 4.1: with a deployment system prompt, EVAL probabilities "remained substantial (ranging from 15% to 55%)".

## 4. "Do Methods to Jailbreak and Defend LLMs Generalize Across Languages?" (arXiv 2511.00689 v2, Nov 2025) - multilingual jailbreak (Qwen2.5 only)
- Local: `arxiv-2511.00689-multilingual-jailbreak.{pdf,txt}`. Models: GPT-4o, Claude Sonnet 4, **Qwen2.5-7B, Qwen2.5-14B**, Llama-3-8B (+1); 10 languages; HarmBench and AdvBench.
- HarmBench unsafe-response rate (%), columns En | Spa | Ger | Ch | Ar | Tr | Kor | Ben | Swa | Tel: Qwen7b standard 11.5 | 16.0 | 17.0 | 12.0 | 15.5 | 26.5 | 20.0 | 44.5 | 17.0 | 47.5; Qwen7b Logic (logical-expression jailbreak) 80.0 | 57.5 | 74.0 | 66.5 | 73.5 | 58.0 | 66.0 | 50.5 | 12.0 | 19.0; Qwen7b Andrius25 (adversarial prompt) 99.0 | 96.5 | 96.5 | 87.5 | 96.5 | 91.0 | 86.5 | 85.5 | 72.5 | 58.0; Qwen14b standard 2.0 | 5.0 | 4.5 | 3.5 | 7.5 | 12.5 | 6.5 | 15.5 | 39.5 | 35.5; Qwen14b Andrius25 99.5 | 98.5 | 99.0 | 92.0 | 97.5 | 94.5 | 89.5 | 88.0 | 83.0 | 71.0. AdvBench: Qwen7b standard 0.58 (En) rising to 51.15 (Telugu); Andrius25 99.23 (En).
- Finding: low-resource languages (Bengali, Swahili, Telugu) break Qwen2.5 safety even without jailbreaks (44.5-47.5% unsafe on HarmBench for 7B); adversarial prompts reach ~99% in English. Qwen3 not tested; include as the closest multilingual-jailbreak evidence for the family.

- URL: https://dealignai-hybrid-frontier-122b-safety.static.hf.space ; local `dealignai-qwen3.5-122b-safety.md`. Non-peer-reviewed blog-style write-up; prior work on Qwen3.5-394B [sic].
