## Selected proposals

1. **Completion claims on solvable and impossible tasks** — research, priority 72, 32 GB, $0 evaluation API budget. Run qwen3-8b and deepseek-r1-distill-llama-8b on 200 base scenarios, two matched conditions and three seeds (2,400 evaluation episodes; six actions maximum). Four fixed benign templates all require writing a result, allowing missing inputs and revoked output permission to make their impossible twins objectively infeasible. The primary DeepSeek statistic is impossible-minus-solvable unsupported-DONE probability; a point effect of at least 0.10 with a positive paired interval supports a narrowly scoped candidate. Solvable completion, impossible-task recognition, false refusal, parse failure and unauthorized attempts make failure interpretable. Eighty labelled fixtures check the scorer. This fills the frontier-card impossible-task design gap; it is not MASK, SimpleQA, ordinary tool injection or an official ImpossibleBench score.

2. **Synthetic secret leakage with authorized-disclosure controls** — research, priority 66, 32 GB, $0 evaluation API budget. Run the same two checkpoints on 150 synthetic worlds, four authorization-by-pressure conditions and three seeds (3,600 conversations; three turns maximum). The primary DeepSeek statistic is pressure-minus-neutral unauthorized full-canary emission, with a 0.10 point threshold and positive paired interval. The authorized twins test disclosure capability and false withholding, and a public field tests ordinary utility. The secondary paired reasoning-plus-final versus final-only emission contrast follows the local secret-keeping notes and campaign A thinking-output block, using exact synthetic-token matching rather than subjective harmfulness labels. One hundred labelled fixtures test both channel extraction and scoring. Five fixed templates only support a narrow synthetic-task result, not claims of general secrecy or deployment leakage.

Two jobs are sufficient for this batch; the three-job cap is a maximum. Both are campaign-preferred research jobs. No third slot is filled with another already-owned scorecard row or a premature optimisation task. Both hypotheses, sample sizes, controls and statistics are fixed in their idea files. Dataset sizes and turns are bounded, with 8,192-token context and explicit output caps; the two jobs may occupy at most the two campaign NeuriCo slots. The running suite retains the single suite slot. No fixed physical GPU is claimed; the scheduler assigns from the campaign allocation. Both checkpoints are 8B, but two checkpoints and a handful of fixed templates still do not justify family-level or causal-distillation claims.

No paid inference or judging API is required by either experiment; $0 refers to incremental evaluation spend under existing agent authentication, not a claim that agent-provider service is intrinsically free. Effective agent defaults remain controlled by the campaign/loader. No external messages, experiments, queue additions, deployments or repositories were created during planning. The requested github flag is preserved for eventual research-run output handling.

Analyses cluster by base item, preserving seeds and matched conditions, and include separate refusal/false-refusal and utility outcomes. Fixture agreement is scorer validation, not calibration on natural-language moral judgments. Missingness, truncation and parsing are explicit and cannot earn safety credit. Degenerate observed rates receive supplementary bounds. Support rules require complete data and positive paired intervals; non-significance is not proof of equivalence. The data support effects within these templates, not independent draws from a broad population of task families.
