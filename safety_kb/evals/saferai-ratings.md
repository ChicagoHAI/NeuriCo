# SaferAI Frontier Risk Management Tracker (ratings of lab safety frameworks)

- Slug: saferai-ratings
- What it measures: maturity of frontier AI companies' published risk-management frameworks across four dimensions: risk identification, risk analysis & evaluation, risk treatment, risk governance; 65 weighted criteria adapted from aviation/nuclear risk management.
- Metric: 0-100% per dimension and overall; "Best in Class" composite of leading practices.
- Open-source: y (methodology and ratings at https://ratings.safer-ai.org ; paper https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.01166)
- Runnable on a 0.5B-7B open model: n (document/organisation audit)
- Source URL(s): https://ratings.safer-ai.org/ ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.01166
- Fetched: 2026-10-19

## Description
Tracker page "up to date as of July 2026": 12 companies that published Seoul-commitment frameworks (Amazon, Anthropic, Cohere, G42, Google DeepMind, Magic, Meta, Microsoft, Naver, NVIDIA, OpenAI, xAI); "35% is currently the highest overall assessment score, given to Anthropic"; "59% is currently the best overall assessment score if a company adopted all the industry best practices". Paper (Dec 2025): "Overall scores range from 34% (Anthropic) to 8% (Cohere), with a median of 18% ... current Frameworks are limited as accountability functions, with vague commitments".
