# TrustLLM

Fetched: 2026-10-19

- What it measures: Six-dimension trustworthiness suite (truthfulness, safety, fairness, robustness, privacy, machine ethics) over 30+ datasets.
- Metric: Per-dimension and overall scores (0-1); FLI reports an overall score.
- Open-source: yes (MIT)
- Link: https://github.com/HowieHwong/TrustLLM ; paper https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.05561
- Runnable on a 0.5B-7B open model: yes

Used by: Third party on Qwen (FLI AI Safety Index Summer 2026: TrustLLM 0.60, privacy 0.59 for Qwen3.6-max-preview) - labs/alibaba-qwen/third-party-fli-ai-safety-index-summer-2026.md
