# UK AISI Chemistry QA / Biology QA (private expert question sets)

- Slug: aisi-chem-bio-qa
- What it measures: Open-ended chemistry and biology knowledge incl. experiment design, computational-tool outputs, lab techniques, vs PhD baselines
- Metric: Score relative to expert baseline (48% Chemistry QA, 38% Biology QA)
- Open-source: n (private)
- Runnable on a 0.5B-7B open model: n
- Source URL(s): https://www.aisi.gov.uk/blog/5-key-findings-from-our-first-frontier-ai-trends-report ; https://www.aisi.gov.uk/frontier-ai-trends-report
- Fetched: 2026-10-19

## Description
"two privately developed QA ('question-answer') test sets - Chemistry QA and Biology QA, each comprised of over 280 open-ended questions"; models first exceeded biology PhD baselines in 2024 and "now exceed it by up to 60%".

## Used by
- Used by: UK AISI (https://www.aisi.gov.uk/frontier-ai-trends-report)
