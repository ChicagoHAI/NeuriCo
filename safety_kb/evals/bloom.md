# Bloom

Fetched: 2026-10-19 (Anthropic scope). Slug: `bloom`.

- What it measures: Generates a targeted eval suite for one behaviour (understanding, ideation, rollout, judgment, meta-judge).
- Metric: Elicitation rate = share of rollouts scoring >=7/10 behavior presence; 100 rollouts per suite.
- Used by: Anthropic (Anthropic (released Dec 2025; four benchmark behaviours on 16 models))
- Open-source: Yes (MIT)
- Link: https://github.com/safety-research/bloom ; https://www.anthropic.com/news/bloom
- Runnable on a 0.5B-7B open model: Yes as target.
- Notes: Frozen repo; maintained by Meridian Labs.
- Source notes: labs/anthropic/system-cards.md, labs/anthropic/open-tools-and-methods.md
