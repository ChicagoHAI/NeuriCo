# Apollo Research ML R&D sabotage capability evaluations

- Slug: apollo-ml-rd-sabotage-capability-evals
- What it measures: Capability to covertly sabotage AI research when deployed for autonomous ML R&D: "four offensive ML sabotage evaluations, spanning three difficulty levels (easy, medium, hard)" (p.71).
- Metric: Success vs human baselines (not quantified in report).
- Open-source: n (Apollo internal)
- Runnable on a 0.5B-7B open model: n (not public; small models unlikely to register).

## Description
Apollo evaluated Gemini 4 Argon "over 10 business days". "Gemini 4 Argon is close to saturating all but the hardest setting in their evaluation suite, substantially exceeding human baselines. However, Apollo pointed out that sabotaging ML R&D would require covertly completing tasks that are substantially harder than those evaluated ... the lack of headroom in this evaluation lowered their confidence" (p.71).
