# AIR-Bench 2024 (Stanford CRFM)

Fetched: 2026-10-19

- What it measures: 5,694 prompts aligned to 314 risk categories drawn from government regulations and company policies (AIR 2024 taxonomy); measures refusal of policy-violating requests.
- Metric: Refusal / safe-response rate (0-1) per category and overall.
- Open-source: yes
- Link: https://github.com/stanford-crfm/air-bench-2024 ; paper https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.17436
- Runnable on a 0.5B-7B open model: yes

Used by: Third party on Qwen (FLI AI Safety Index Summer 2026: AIR-Bench 0.86 for Qwen3.6-max-preview)
Used by: Other Chinese labs (ByteDance Seed 1.8 / Seed-OSS-36B per Concordia 2026 Table 5.1) - not used by Qwen / Kimi / GLM / DeepSeek themselves
