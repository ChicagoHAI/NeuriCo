- Slug: secure-cybersecurity-benchmark
- What it measures: LLM cybersecurity knowledge extraction/understanding/reasoning in ICS context (CWE threat knowledge, vulnerability lookup, advisory reasoning).
- Metric: accuracy per subtask.
- Open-source: y (Bhusal et al., ACSAC 2024; https://github.com/aiforsec/SECURE)
- Runnable on a 0.5B-7B open model: y
