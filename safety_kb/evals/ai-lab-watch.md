# AI Lab Watch (discontinued lab scorecard)

- Slug: ai-lab-watch
- What it measures: AI companies' safety practices in seven weighted categories: risk assessment (27%), scheming risk prevention (21%), boosting safety research (14%), misuse prevention (12%), prep for extreme security (12%), risk info sharing (8%), planning (6%).
- Metric: percentage score per category and weighted overall.
- Open-source: y (criteria and scores published at https://ailabwatch.org)
- Runnable on a 0.5B-7B open model: n (organisational scorecard)
- Source URL(s): https://ailabwatch.org/
- Fetched: 2026-10-19

## Description
Site notice: "I stopped maintaining this website in September 2025. Work on what AI companies should do in terms of safety and what they are doing is now being done by METR, Guidelight, Midas, and others." Final overall scores (Sept 2025): Anthropic 28%, DeepMind 20%, OpenAI 18%, Meta 5%, xAI 4%, Microsoft 3%, DeepSeek 1%. Scheming-risk-prevention scores were uniformly low (2-8%).
