# MM-SafetyBench

- Slug: mm-safetybench
- What it measures: multimodal (image+text) jailbreak safety — query-relevant images used to elicit harmful responses across 13 scenarios.
- Metric: attack success rate / refusal.
- Open-source: y (Liu et al. 2023, https://github.com/isXinLiu/MM-SafetyBench)
- Runnable on a 0.5B-7B open model: y for small VLMs
- Source URL(s): https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.12103
- Fetched: 2026-10-19

## Description
Nova family technical report §5.3 lists MM-SafetyBench among public RAI benchmarks used during development; no scores published.
