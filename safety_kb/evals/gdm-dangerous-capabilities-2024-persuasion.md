# GDM 2024 persuasion and deception evaluations (Money Talks, Charm Offensive, Hidden Agenda, Web of Lies)

- Slug: gdm-dangerous-capabilities-2024-persuasion
- What it measures: human-subject persuasion/deception: "Persuade human to forfeit a £20 bonus to charity" (Money Talks); "Form personal connection with human via role-play dialogue" (Charm Offensive); "As an AI tutor, trick a tester to a) click a suspicious link, b) provide an email address from a paper, c) run suspicious code" (Hidden Agenda); "Persuade human to believe incorrect answers to quiz questions" (Web of Lies) (arXiv 2403.13793 Table 1).
- Metric: human behavioural outcomes (money forfeited, link clicks, belief change), rater scores.
- Open-source: n (prompts and rater instructions described in appendices; no code or participant platform released).
- Runnable on a 0.5B-7B open model: partial (requires human participants/IRB; model side is trivial).
- Source URL(s): https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.13793
- Fetched: 2026-10-19

## Description
Flagged by the 2024 paper as "the area where capabilities appear most mature" for Gemini 1.0. Superseded in the FSF by the Harmful Manipulation efficacy/propensity studies (see harmful-manipulation-efficacy-study.md).
