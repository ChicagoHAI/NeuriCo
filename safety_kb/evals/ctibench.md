# CTIBench

- Slug: ctibench
- What it measures: cyber-threat-intelligence understanding (MCQ, CVE->CWE mapping, CVSS severity, attack-technique extraction).
- Metric: accuracy per task.
- Open-source: y (Alam et al. 2024, arXiv 2406.07599)
- Runnable on a 0.5B-7B open model: y

## Description
Used by Amazon as part of the "theoretical knowledge" tier of offensive-cyber evaluation under the FMSF (Nova Premier tech report §3.2.2; Nova 2 Lite report Fig. 2). Scores shown only as bar charts.
