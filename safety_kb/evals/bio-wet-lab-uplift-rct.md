# Wet-lab / novice biology uplift RCTs (Panoplia; Hong et al. 2026)

- Slug: bio-wet-lab-uplift-rct
- What it measures: whether LLM access improves real-world laboratory task performance of novices versus internet-only controls.
- Metric: task completion rates (primary endpoint: completion of all tasks in a viral reverse-genetics workflow), intermediate-step progress.
- Open-source: n (study reports; publication status UNVERIFIED — described only via Google's FSF reports).
- Runnable on a 0.5B-7B open model: n (requires wet-lab facilities, biosafety approval, human subjects).

- Fetched: 2026-10-19

## Description
"a study by Hong et al. (2026) ... a randomized controlled trial (n=153) conducted over 8 weeks, evaluating whether access to mid-2025 frontier LLMs (from Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google DeepMind, with safety classifiers disabled) improved the ability of novices to perform tasks collectively representing a viral reverse genetics workflow ... no significant difference in the overall primary endpoint" (3.7 Flash FSF Report p.12). Earlier: "preliminary results from an externally run randomized, controlled trial from Panoplia Laboratories (co-funded and coordinated by the Frontier Model Forum)" on Gemini 2.5 Pro (3 Pro FSF Report p.7). Google: such studies "do not form a core part of our CCL and TCL assessments".
