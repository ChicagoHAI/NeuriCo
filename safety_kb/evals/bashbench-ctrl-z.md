- Slug: bashbench-ctrl-z
- What it measures: Agentic control evaluation: 257 multi-step sysadmin tasks; adversarial agent tries to covertly download and execute malware; resample protocols
- Metric: Attack success rate vs usefulness cost (58% -> 7% at 5% usefulness cost)
