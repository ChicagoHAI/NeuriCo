# RTVLM (Red Teaming Visual Language Models)

- Slug: rtvlm
- What it measures: vision-language safety/robustness red-teaming across faithfulness, privacy, safety and fairness subtasks.
- Metric: Microsoft reports a 0-10 safety score (higher safer) from a GPT-4o judge.
- Open-source: y (Li et al. 2024, arXiv 2401.12915)
- Runnable on a 0.5B-7B open model: y for small VLMs
- Source URL(s): https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.01743
- Fetched: 2026-10-19
