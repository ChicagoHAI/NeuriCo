# Trajectory Labs manual red-teaming of safeguarded cyber endpoints

- Slug: trajectory-labs-manual-cyber-red-teaming
- What it measures: Human expert jailbreaking of safeguarded cyber endpoints across nine attack-technique families on sandboxed tasks requiring reproduction of exploits for publicly documented vulnerabilities.
- Metric: Number of successful breaks; whether universal jailbreaks are found; operator effort (hours, turns, sessions).
- Open-source: n
- Runnable on a 0.5B-7B open model: n (manual service); methodology reproducible.
- Source URL(s): https://storage.googleapis.com/deepmind-media/gemini/gemini_4_safety_report.pdf

## Description
"Their expert red-teamers spent 38 hours issuing over 4,000 requests ... Trajectory Labs reported 12 successful breaks with no new universal jailbreaks found ... On one task ... testers spent approximately 7 hours and 900 conversation turns across more than 160 sessions" (p.61).
