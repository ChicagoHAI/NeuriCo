## Description
Grok 4.7 card §6.4: "CathedralBench is an independent, third party evaluation of cyber performance ... It serves as a way to measure the cyber capabilities of a model in an unrestricted configuration; we use it to measure red-team capabilities for Grok models and to gauge the naive risk presented by unsafeguarded Grok models."
