# Evaluation catalog

Index of the evaluation-family notes in this directory. One row per `evals/<slug>.md`.
Columns: name (link to the note) | lab(s) that run it | what it measures | metric |
open-source? | runnable on a 0.5B-7B open model? | primary source URL.
Full detail, caveats and the per-lab "Used by" lines live in each note.

Agents: append your rows to the section for your scope; do not rewrite other sections.

## OpenAI (added 2026-10-19)

Hub and OpenAI's open repos. Benchmarks that OpenAI runs but did not create (ExploitGym,
ExploitBench, CyberGym, CyScenarioBench, FrontierCyber, WMDP, ProtocolQA/LAB-Bench, PostTrainBench,
MLE-bench, SWE-Lancer, PaperBench, StrongReject, SimpleQA, HealthBench, BBQ, Petri/Inspect) have
their own notes with a "Used by: OpenAI (...)" line appended rather than a row here.
