# FLI AI Safety Index (lab scorecard)

- Slug: fli-ai-safety-index
- What it measures: safety practices of frontier AI companies (not models) across six domains: Risk Assessment, Current Harms, Safety Frameworks, Existential Safety, Governance & Accountability, Information Sharing (37 indicators in 2026), graded by an independent expert panel.
- Metric: letter grade A-F and numeric score (0-4 scale) per company and per domain; averaged across reviewers.
- Open-source: partial (full reports, indicators and methodology published; reviewer-level grades confidential) https://futureoflife.org/ai-safety-index-summer-2026/
- Runnable on a 0.5B-7B open model: n (organisational audit)
- Source URL(s): https://futureoflife.org/ai-safety-index-summer-2025/ ; https://futureoflife.org/ai-safety-index-winter-2025/ ; https://futureoflife.org/ai-safety-index-summer-2026/
- Fetched: 2026-10-19
